# Stillpoint AI — Google Meet Companion Extension (Phase 2)

This Chrome Extension injects a real-time behavioral telemetry heads-up display directly into **Google Meet (`meet.google.com`)**.

---

## How to Install in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

1. Open your browser and navigate to:
   - Chrome: `chrome://extensions/`
   - Edge: `edge://extensions/`
2. In the top right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button.
4. Select the `extension` folder located at:
   `c:\Users\Mohit Koteshwar Puni\OneDrive\Desktop\MY FILES\New camera system\extension`
5. The extension **"Stillpoint AI — Google Meet Interview Companion"** is now installed!

---

## How to Use During an Interview

1. Join any meeting on **[meet.google.com](https://meet.google.com)**.
2. The Stillpoint AI companion HUD will automatically attach in the top-right corner of Google Meet.
3. It detects the candidate's video tile and continuously displays:
   - **Live Focus & Integrity Score** (0–100%)
   - **Ocular Gaze Vector** (*Center, Left, Right, Down*)
   - **Head Posture**
   - **Saccade Reading Pattern Alerts**
4. Click **🚩 Flag Moment** at any time during an answer to mark suspicious behavior.
5. Click **📊 Full Suite** or open `http://localhost:8000` to see the synchronized master dashboard with deep radar breakdowns and exportable PDF/JSON reports!
