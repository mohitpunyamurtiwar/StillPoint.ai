01 / Executive Summary & Product Thesis

In modern remote hiring and academic examinations, video conferencing tools (Google Meet, Zoom, MS Teams) have become the default venue for evaluating technical competence, critical thinking, and communication skills. However, the rapid proliferation of Large Language Models (LLMs) and invisible teleprompter software has caused an existential crisis for remote interviews: candidates routinely read generated code solutions and AI-crafted answers from off-screen monitors or discreet browser overlays while feigning natural camera gaze.

Legacy proctoring software has failed to solve this challenge. Platforms such as Proctorio, Honorlock, and HireVue require intrusive root-level system access, install surveillance bloatware, stream raw video to remote clouds (triggering severe GDPR and biometric privacy lawsuits), and rely on coarse thresholds that generate endless false positives.

The Stillpoint AI Thesis: High-integrity interview telemetry does not require cloud video surveillance. By executing dual lightweight neural networks locally within the client browser via WebGL and WebAssembly, Stillpoint AI extracts millisecond-precision 3D biometrics, micro-saccadic teleprompter patterns, and environmental cues—delivering continuous behavioral clarity to interviewers in real-time with zero cloud data liability.

02 / The 4 "World-First" Pioneer Factors

Stillpoint AI introduces four technical and architectural breakthroughs that differentiate it from every existing assessment, interview, or proctoring tool on the market:

Pillar 1 Zero-Cloud In-Browser Dual-Neural Engine

Unlike platforms that stream candidate video to AWS/Azure for server-side processing, Stillpoint loads both MediaPipe FaceMesh (478 3D landmarks with refined iris) and TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD directly into browser GPU memory via WebGL. 100% of telemetry extraction occurs locally on the interviewer's or candidate's machine at 25–30 FPS with zero cloud bandwidth costs.

Pillar 2 Micro-Saccadic AI Script & Teleprompter Detection

Candidates using ChatGPT or secondary monitors look toward the screen and read text while keeping their heads static. Legacy proctors only measure coarse head yaw (>25°). Stillpoint introduces an algorithmic saccade detector (_detectReadingSaccade) that detects horizontal sweeping motions and rapid eye jumps characteristic of reading off-screen notes.

Pillar 3 Spatial Dynamic Zonal Motion Decomposition

Legacy motion sensors flag any pixel change as cheating, penalizing candidates who gesticulate naturally while talking. Stillpoint dynamically segments the video into three spatial zones: Face Box, Torso & Gesture Zone, and Peripheral Room Border. Hand-to-chin gestures and arm movements are validated as natural communication, while true room intrusions trigger notices.

Pillar 4 Ephemeral Zero-Biometric-Liability Architecture

With strict biometric data regulations (EU AI Act, Illinois BIPA, GDPR), holding facial recordings creates massive corporate liability. Stillpoint operates an ephemeral pipeline: video frames are analyzed in volatile RAM and immediately overwritten. Only abstract telemetry events (e.g., timestamps, head angles) are logged. Zero biometric face templates are ever stored.

04 / Deep Neural ML Engine (ml-engine.js)

At the core of Stillpoint is ml-engine.js, an ultra-optimized client-side computer vision engine designed to run at 30+ FPS without causing UI thread stutter or frame dropping.

1. 3D Head Pose Matrix Estimation (SolvePnP Approximation)

Traditional 2D facial landmarking cannot accurately isolate 3D spatial rotation. Stillpoint uses 6 canonical anatomical landmarks to construct a 3D orthogonal coordinate system:

  • Nose Tip (Landmark 1) & Subnasale
  • Menton / Chin Tip (Landmark 152)
  • Left Eye Lateral Canthus (33) & Right Eye Lateral Canthus (263)
  • Left Lip Corner (61) & Right Lip Corner (291)

The engine calculates the Euclidean center of the facial plane and computes Euler angles:

Yaw (θ_y) = atan2(Δx_nose, Δz_depth) · (180/π)
Pitch (θ_p) = atan2(Δy_nose - Δy_center, Δz_depth) · (180/π)
Roll (θ_r) = atan2(y_right_eye - y_left_eye, x_right_eye - x_left_eye) · (180/π)

To prevent micro-jitter caused by sensor noise, angles are filtered using a temporal Exponential Moving Average (EMA) with frame-rate adaptive alpha:

Smoothed_Angle_t = α · Angle_raw + (1 - α) · Smoothed_Angle_{t-1}     [α = 0.35]

2. Ocular Biometrics & Iris Vectoring

By utilizing MediaPipe's refined Iris landmarks (indices 468–472 for the left iris and 473–477 for the right iris), Stillpoint measures precise pupil displacement relative to the eye contours:

  • Horizontal Gaze Ratio (H_ratio): Distance from iris center to medial canthus divided by total palpebral fissure width. Neutral gaze is normalized to 0.50.
  • Vertical Gaze Ratio (V_ratio): Distance from iris center to superior eyelid margin divided by vertical aperture.

3. Micro-Saccadic Teleprompter / AI Reading Detection

When a person reads text from an on-screen or off-screen teleprompter, their eyes exhibit a distinctive oculomotor signature: slow continuous horizontal tracking sweeps interrupted by rapid ballistic saccadic repositioning jumps back to the start of the next line.

_detectReadingSaccade(history): Rapid_Jumps ≥ 2 & Sweeping_Motions ≥ 6 over 18 frames & |Avg_Gaze| ≥ 15°

When this periodic oculomotor condition persists, Stillpoint triggers an immediate alert: "Ocular Saccade: Teleprompter / Notes Reading Pattern Detected".

5. Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) & Fatigue / Ptosis Detection

Alertness and drowsiness are tracked via the clinical Eye Aspect Ratio formula applied across six upper and lower eyelid landmarks:

EAR = ( ||p2 - p6|| + ||p3 - p5|| ) / ( 2 · ||p1 - p4|| )

A normal open eye yields 0.28–0.34 EAR. A full blink drops below 0.18 EAR. If EAR drops below 0.21 for more than 1.4 seconds, Stillpoint flags an alert for Eyelid Ptosis & Fatigue, distinguishing natural blinking from sleepiness.

6. Spatial Dynamic Zonal Motion & Object Surveillance

To prevent false positives from hand gesturing during natural speech, Stillpoint renders each frame into a lightweight off-screen 96×72 analysis canvas and applies spatial zonal luminance difference:

In parallel, TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD scans every 600ms for unauthorized objects: mobile phones (cell phone), external laptops, tablets, or secondary persons entering the frame.

05 / Behavioral Evaluation Engine (ai-evaluator.js)

When an interview concludes or the interviewer requests an interim review, the raw telemetry timeline is synthesized by ai-evaluator.js into a 5-dimensional cognitive index.

The 5 Behavioral Evaluation Dimensions

1. Cognitive Steadiness & Focal Lock (30% Weight)

Measures gaze fixation, sustained focus on the primary screen, and absence of off-screen glance drift or teleprompter saccades.

2. Environmental Integrity & Compliance (30% Weight)

Evaluates unauthorized mobile devices, secondary individuals, room background intrusions, and off-camera whisper spikes.

3. Affective Composure & Stress Resilience (15% Weight)

Monitors blink rate spikes (>28/min), facial micro-twitches, brow furrowing under technical questioning, and affective steadiness.

4. Physical Demeanor & Alertness (15% Weight)

Tracks postural stability, forward engagement, eyelid aperture (EAR), and absence of fatigue, slouching, or drowsiness.

5. Communicative Confidence & Articulation (10% Weight)

Analyzes balanced speaking-to-listening ratio (target 25%–65% active dialogue), facial engagement, and responsive eyebrow cues.

Mathematical Formulation of the Composite Index

Composite_Score = (Cognitive · 0.30) + (Integrity · 0.30) + (Composure · 0.15) + (Demeanor · 0.15) + (Communication · 0.10)

06 / Google Meet Extension & Interviewer UX

To eliminate the need for candidates to install proprietary software, Stillpoint includes a Google Chrome / Edge Manifest V3 Companion Extension (extension/) that directly augments live Google Meet sessions.

Extension Architecture & Meet DOM Hooking

  • Zero Candidate Burden: The interview takes place on standard meet.google.com. The candidate joins using their standard browser without installing any third-party app or extension.
  • Interviewer Draggable Floating HUD: The extension injects an unobtrusive, translucent HUD into the interviewer's Google Meet tab (extension/content.js). The HUD displays real-time Focus Score, Gaze Direction, Saccade Notes status, and a one-click 🚩 Flag Moment button.
  • Cross-Context Coordinator Sync (BroadcastChannel): The injected Meet script and the master Stillpoint Dashboard communicate asynchronously via the HTML5 BroadcastChannel("stillpoint_ai_coordinator") API, allowing the interviewer to view deep metrics on a second screen while keeping the HUD visible in Meet.

07 / Privacy, Ethics & Complete Codebase Blueprint

Ethical AI Principles & Regulatory Compliance

Stillpoint AI was engineered specifically to adhere to global AI governance frameworks:

  • EU Artificial Intelligence Act (High-Risk AI Systems): Stillpoint enforces human-in-the-loop oversight. All algorithmic scores are presented as unverified heuristic prompts for human review, never automated hire/reject decisions.
  • Biometric Privacy Laws (e.g., Illinois BIPA, GDPR Article 9): Because zero facial embeddings or video frames are saved to disk or transmitted to cloud endpoints, organizations eliminate exposure to biometric privacy lawsuits.
  • Neurodivergence & Physical Diversity Safeguard: The calibration baseline allows candidates with involuntary motor tics, strabismus, or varied physical baselines to calibrate their neutral center, preventing false penalization.
