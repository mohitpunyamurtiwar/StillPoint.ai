SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Stillpoint AI Coordinator

1. Google Meet & Screen Stream Coordination:
Using the HTML5 Screen Capture API (getDisplayMedia), Stillpoint receives candidate video directly from Google Meet without requiring software installation or invasive device drivers.

2. Machine Learning Engine:
- MediaPipe FaceMesh & Iris: 478 3D landmarks, eye aspect ratios, iris gaze vectors, and head pose orientation matrix.
- TensorFlow COCO-SSD: Object detection for phones, laptops, and multiple faces in frame.

3. Privacy Guarantee:
All neural networks execute locally in your browser via WebGL and WebAssembly SIMD. No raw video frames are saved to disk or transmitted across the internet.
